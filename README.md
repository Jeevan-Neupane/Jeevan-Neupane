## What I'm Working On

### Shruti

At A.I. Lab, we are building **Shruti**, an AI clinical documentation assistant.

It listens to doctor-patient conversations and helps convert them into structured clinical notes.

Some of the problems I am currently working on:

- Nepali-English code-mixed speech recognition
- Medical terminology recognition
- Clinical note generation
- Speaker handling in doctor-patient conversations
- Noisy clinical audio
- ASR model fine-tuning
- EMR and hospital system integration
- Smaller and more affordable AI models for deployment

**Current stack**

## Current Research

### Nepali-English Medical ASR

One of my main research areas right now is speech recognition for Nepali-English medical conversations.

Real clinical conversations are quite different from normal speech datasets. Doctors and patients switch between Nepali and English, use many medical terms, speak in noisy rooms and sometimes speak over each other.

I am currently experimenting with models such as:

`IndicConformer` `FastConformer` `Whisper` `XLS-R`

I mainly evaluate them using:

`WER` `CER` `Medical Term Accuracy` `RTF`

I am also working on dataset cleaning, transcription quality, fine-tuning and benchmarking.
